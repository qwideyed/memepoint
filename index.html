The Great Epochs of Aetheria

Epoch I: The Dawning Spires (Year 0 - 1,200)

The Solar Synthesis

The first human navigators arrive at the Binary Rim and discover the resonant singing crystals of Aethel. Ten colossal spires are raised around the primary star to channel unlimited solar harmony.

Epoch II: The Fracture (Year 2,450)

Shattering of the Firmament

An overcharged solar nexus creates the Great Spatial Rift, splitting the system into fragmented sovereign sectors. House Aurum and the Shadow Syndicate wage war over the lingering celestial beacons.

Epoch III: The Concord (Year 3,900 - Present)

Establishment of the Imperial Codex

Under the decree of Queen Seraphina and the Arch-Scribe conclave, the Imperial Archives are consecrated to record every remaining celestial fragment and prevent forbidden solar exploitation.
